# Durga visarjan 2026: ghat water brief, Bhopal

For the BMC officer planning Durga visarjan, 20 October 2026. Figures from the MPPCB visarjan reports 2023-2025.

## Basis
7 ghats, Ganesh and Durga, tested before, during and a week after immersion, against CPCB Class B (outdoor bathing): DO at least 5 mg/l, BOD at most 3 mg/l, pH 6.5 to 8.5. A screening yardstick, not a legal finding. Score 0-100: how often and how far readings miss these limits. 2023-2025 history only; no 2026 results exist. Units are assumed (not printed).

## Overall
- DO below 5 mg/l in 42 of 116 readings; 4 read 0, all at Bairagarh.
- BOD above 3 mg/l in 100 of 112.
- pH outside range in 6 of 115.
- DO was already below 5 mg/l before immersion in 12 of 38 readings, so these figures alone do not show immersion as the cause.

## Highest scores
1. Bairagarh (Upper Lake): score 47.5 (range 34.3-62.1), rank 1-2. DO low 11/13, BOD high 13/13. Lowest DO 0 mg/l (4 times, e.g. 2023 Ganesh after, 2023 report p.26).
2. Malikhedi (Kund): score 34.7 (range 25.1-52.9), rank 1-6. DO low 9/13, BOD high 11/13. Lowest DO 0.6 mg/l (2025 Ganesh during, 2025 report p.29).
3. Shahpura (Shahpura Lake): score 32.0, rank 2-3. DO low 5/18, BOD high 18/18. Lowest DO 2.5 mg/l (2025 Durga pre, 2025 report p.65).

## Durga readings only
1 Bairagarh, 2 Shahpura, 3 Malikhedi, 4 Khatlapura, 5 Rani Kamlapati, 6 Hathaikheda, 7 Prempura.

## Data gaps
- 17 DO/BOD/pH readings unsampled (Bairagarh, Khatlapura, Malikhedi, Prempura); no Durga 2025 table for Bairagarh and Malikhedi. Never counted as clean.
- Metals not scored (no Class B limit); total coliform not tested.
- Rani Kamlapati / Kamla Park may be two places.
- Weights are our judgment: across 6 weightings Bairagarh is first in 5 and Prempura last in 5; ranks 3-6 reorder.

## Use
A guide for where to send extra cleaning, aeration and water testing. The decision is the officer's. No health effects are claimed.
